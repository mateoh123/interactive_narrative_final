The fire engulfed my house. What now?

This was the reality of hundreds of Altadena, California residents on January 7th, 2025.
Dubbed the 'Eaton Fire,' it was one of the most devastating wildfires in Calfornia's history.

Amid extreme drought and the hurricane-force Santa Ana winds, the Eaton Fire burned for 24 days before being fully contained on January 31st.

60% of Altadena burned, resulting in the displacement of more than 200,000 people.
